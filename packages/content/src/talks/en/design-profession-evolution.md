title: "From Vitruvius to design engineer: 2,000 years of a profession's evolution"
---

What do an ancient architect, a Renaissance artist, a 20th-century industrial designer and today's AI designer have in common?

At first glance, not that much. But look at the history of creative professions through technological, cultural and social change, and some curious patterns emerge.

In this lecture we'll look at how the demands on creators changed, why some professions appeared while others disappeared, and try to understand where design is heading today.

Perhaps the future of our profession is much closer to its distant past than it seems.
