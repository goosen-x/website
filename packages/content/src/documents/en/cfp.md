Tell us what you would like to talk about. No experience needed: the organisers help with the topic, the structure and a rehearsal. Before you apply, have a look at the [speaker guide](https://css.moscow/en/speaker-guide/).

The form has no name field: we will write to the contact you give. Only the organisers see the proposal, and how we handle the data is described in the [forms section of the policy](https://css.moscow/en/privacy/).
