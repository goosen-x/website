## Definitions

1. Program committee (PC): the administrators of the channel [@moscowcss](https://t.me/moscowcss), listed in the channel description
2. Participant: a legally capable individual who wishes to speak at the conference
3. Personal data: last name, first name, and a contact: a phone number or a link to a social network
4. Meetup: a gathering of participants with prepared talks and of listeners

## Policy and rules

1. The participant provides personal data in person to the MoscowCSS meetup program committee before the talk is approved for the program of the nearest meetup. The data is not passed on to third parties.
2. Personal data is processed to put together the meetup program and to contact the prospective speaker.
3. After approval, personal data may be passed on to the partner organization of the conference, on whose premises the meetup will be held.
4. Once the partner is chosen, the MoscowCSS PC will ask you for consent again.
5. The data is provided for a period of at least 25 years and can be withdrawn by the applicant by contacting the channel [@moscowcss](https://t.me/moscowcss).

## Forms on the site

This section covers the forms "Propose a talk", "Meetup feedback" and "Ask a question". It applies to the data from these forms instead of the rules above.

1. What is stored. In a talk proposal: a contact (Telegram or email), the title, theses, format, readiness, a comment and the mark of consent. In feedback: ratings and comments, without a name or contacts, so feedback cannot be linked to you. In a question: the text, who it is addressed to and a contact if you gave one.
2. Why. We use a proposal to put together the programme and to contact you. Feedback helps speakers and organisers, and questions are stored to be answered.
3. Spam protection. The form is checked by Cloudflare Turnstile, which processes your browser data under [its own policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
4. Where. The data from the forms is kept on a server in Switzerland.
5. How long. A talk proposal is deleted automatically after 12 months unless the organisers decide to keep it longer, for example because you gave the talk. The retention period of feedback and questions is not set yet, and we will state it here.
6. How to delete. Write to the organisers: [@DreamShaded](https://t.me/DreamShaded), [@RinaPa](https://t.me/RinaPa) or [@antytoto](https://t.me/antytoto), and name the contact from your proposal or question. Feedback is anonymous, so we cannot find and delete yours.
